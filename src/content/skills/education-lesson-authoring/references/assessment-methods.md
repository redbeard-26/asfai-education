# Assessment method selection

- **Conversation:** Ask adaptive prompts from different angles. Store concise demonstrations, not raw transcripts by default.
- **Game telemetry:** Normalize product-specific events deterministically. Retain practice, mechanics, luck, stress, and time confounds.
- **Writing:** Apply versioned criteria to the submitted work, cite the relevant portion, and distinguish coached drafts from independent work.
- **Performance:** Use an attributed teacher/peer observation or submitted media. Never claim to have observed an unseen action.
- **Collaboration:** Record group output and individual contribution separately. Group success alone is not individual mastery.
- **Project:** Consider the artifact, tests or observable behavior, explanation, provenance, and assistance.
- **Self-reflection:** Use for strategy, confidence, and next-step evidence; never use it alone to establish subject-matter mastery.

Prefer multiple evidence types for mastery. Keep the tutor and assessor roles distinguishable and record all meaningful assistance.
